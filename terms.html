Terms of use

Aura, a calisthenics training and assessment app. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Training is at your own risk

Aura suggests exercises, levels and tests. It cannot see you, and it is not medical advice. Warm up, stop when something hurts, and check with a doctor before starting if you have an injury or a health condition. Assessments are meant to be done with a coach present.

Ratings are provisional

Ratings, Aura scores and skill levels come from a model that is still being calibrated. They show your progress inside Aura; they are not an official measure or certification. Results you record yourself are shown as self-reported and kept apart from results a coach observed or verified.

Your account

Record honestly, keep your sign-in to yourself, and do not use Aura to harass or impersonate anyone. Accounts that do may be removed. Your data remains yours; see the privacy page for how it is handled and how to delete it.

The service

Aura is new and changes often. It is provided as it is, without a guarantee that it is always available or free of errors. Features may change or be removed.
